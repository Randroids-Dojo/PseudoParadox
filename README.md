# Pseudo Paradox

A single-room time travel puzzle game where you escape by manipulating your own past selves.

You wake up on the floor of a spaceship with no memory of how you got there. Four doors line the walls. Each is a fixed portal to a different time in the same room. Walk through one and you'll spawn back in the room at another hour, where a future instance of yourself may already be waiting. Every action you take is recorded; once a timeline is written, past instances replay it exactly. You can knock them out, drag their bodies through portals, and coordinate across generations of yourself to escape, but you can never undo what you've already done.

No paradoxes are possible. The confusion is the puzzle.
